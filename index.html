About

Hi, I'm Habib, a recent Duke graduate working across AI and Web3.

During college, I co-authored HKConv, a convolution operator that lets hyperbolic neural networks capture local structure in graph data. It achieved state-of-the-art results on seven benchmarks and was published at LoG 2024. I also won a silver medal at ICPC Chengdu, one of the most competitive contests of the world's premier programming contest.

Recently, I've been building in Web3, focused on how AI agents can trust each other on-chain. I started by tracing every ERC-8004 review on Base to the money behind it, and found far fewer independent sources behind them than the review counts suggest.

I like to work on difficult and interesting problems, and I'm open to joining a team that takes them on. Feel free to reach me on LinkedIn, X, or by email.
